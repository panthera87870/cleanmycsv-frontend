1. Klaviyo SMS Consent Rejections (E.164 Requirement)

Klaviyo SMS requires phone numbers formatted in strict international E.164 standards (e.g. +15550192834 or +33612345678). Unformatted numbers (06.12.34.56.78) cause Klaviyo to skip SMS consent imports entirely. CleanMyCSV cleans formatting, restores dropped zeros, and prepends correct country codes.
